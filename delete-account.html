Delete your account

Voya by Viktor Leongard

A Voya account is optional. You only have one if you turned on cloud sync and signed in with Apple, Google, or an email address. Deleting it removes your account and the copy of your plan stored in the cloud.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Voya and tap the Voya logo at the top of the screen. The Cloud sync sheet opens.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You sign in once more to prove it is you, then the account is deleted.

The plan on your phone stays where it is. The app goes back to working without an account.

Delete it without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email avidity.wands.70@icloud.com from the address you signed in with, or tell us which Apple or Google account you used. We delete the account and its cloud data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: sign-in method, email address and user ID.
  • Your synced plan: vacation and flex days, allowance, overtime, regions and display settings.

Nothing is kept after deletion. If you signed in with Apple, Voya also revokes its access to your Apple ID.

What is not deleted

Data stored only on your device stays there until you remove the app. Advertising data that Google AdMob processes is covered by Google's own policies. See our Privacy Policy.
