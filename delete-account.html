Delete your Voya account

Voya by Viktor Leongard

A Voya account only exists if you signed in for cloud sync. If you never signed in, there is no account and nothing stored outside your device.

In the app

  1. Open Voya and tap the umbrella.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or with Apple or Google when asked.

Your account and the cloud copy of your plan are deleted right away. The plan on your device stays.

Without the app

Email us from the address you signed in with and ask for your account to be deleted:

avidity.wands.70@icloud.com

If you signed in with Apple's "Hide My Email", write from that Apple ID's email and tell us so, and we'll find your account. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, user ID and name, if any.
  • The copy of your plan stored in the cloud: vacation and overtime days, allowance, balances, regions and settings.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Data on your own devices is not affected; uninstall the app to remove it. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which handles its data under Google's policies.
