Frequently asked

How do I book a vacation day?

Tap any day in the Year planner or Months view, then choose to book a vacation day or log flex / overtime. Your remaining balance updates as you plan.

Where do public and school holidays come from?

Holidays for 30+ countries are built in, with regional accuracy. Set your country (and region) in settings to see the right ones.

What are bridge days?

Bridge days are the single days off that connect a public holiday to a weekend — so you get the longest break for the fewest vacation days. Voya highlights them for you.

How do I connect a calendar?

Open the add-calendar sheet and connect Google Calendar or Outlook through their secure sign-in, or paste any .ics / iCal subscription link (including a public Apple Calendar link). Connections are read-only and can be removed anytime.

How does the AI planning assistant work?

Voya creates a ready-made prompt you can copy into your favourite AI assistant to get bridge-day and long-weekend suggestions. Your data isn't sent anywhere by Voya — you choose where to paste the prompt.

Is my data private?

Yes — your plans stay on your device and the app works offline. See our Privacy Policy for details.
