Voya

Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Voya is a vacation and time-off planner that helps you see your whole year, find bridge days, and plan around holidays. We built Voya to be private by design. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

The short version

  • Your plans stay on your device unless you turn on cloud sync.
  • No account or sign-up is required to use Voya. Cloud sync is optional.
  • If you sign in for cloud sync, your plan is stored in your own cloud account so it can sync between your devices. You can delete that account at any time, in the app or by email.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects limited data to serve them.

Information stored on your device

Your vacation days, overtime and balances, calendars you create, events, and your settings are stored locally on your device. Unless you turn on cloud sync, this information is not sent to us or to any server. Removing the app removes this data from the device.

Cloud sync and your account (optional)

From Voya 1.2 you can sign in to keep your plan in sync across your phone and tablet and to keep a backup of it. Signing in is entirely optional; without it, Voya works exactly as before and nothing leaves your device.

If you sign in, we store:

  • Account details: your email address and a user ID. If you sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we only receive Apple's relay address. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we may also receive the name on that account.
  • Your plan: your vacation and overtime days, allowance, overtime balance, working-time settings, regions and app settings.

We use this data only to sync your plan between your devices and restore it after you sign in on a new one. It is not used for advertising or tracking, not sold, and not shared with anyone else.

Accounts and synced data are handled by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), provided by Google as our service provider. Your plan is stored in a database located in the European Union; sign-in data may be processed by Google in other countries under its data-protection terms. Data is encrypted in transit. Only you can read or change your own data.

Calendar events from connected calendars and the text of AI prompts are not synced.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: open the cloud sync sheet and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the copy of your plan stored in the cloud. The plan on your device stays until you remove it or uninstall the app.

If you no longer have the app, you can ask us to delete your account by email. See Delete your Voya account. Signing out alone does not delete anything.

Calendar connections (optional)

If you choose to connect a calendar, Voya uses the provider's official, read-only sign-in:

  • Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook — connected through their secure OAuth sign-in (read-only access). Sign-in tokens are stored securely on your device.
  • iCal / .ics subscriptions (including a public Apple Calendar link) — fetched over the internet from the address you provide.

Calendar events are retrieved to your device to display alongside your plans. They are not transmitted to us. You can disconnect a calendar at any time in the app.

Advertising

Voya displays ads using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as device identifiers and app usage. This processing is governed by Google's policies — see How Google uses information and the AdMob documentation. You can limit ad personalization in your device's privacy settings.

AI planning assistant

Voya's AI feature builds a ready-to-use text prompt on your device that you can copy into the AI assistant of your choice. Voya does not send your data to any AI service. If you paste the prompt into another app, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Voya is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You stay in control: disconnect calendars, clear your plans, sign out of cloud sync, delete your account, or uninstall the app. You can also ask us for a copy of the data stored in your account, or to correct or delete it. For any questions or requests, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
